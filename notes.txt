Bruun FFT references
====================

Bruun 1978 ("z-transform DFT filters and FFT's")
------------------------------------------------
- Describes discrete Fourier transform filter banks that minimize the number of multipliers.
- Introduces FFT forms that use only real coefficients for real signals.
- Claims new FFT algorithms require roughly half the real multiplications of the classical FFT.

CS1 Intro to FFT
----------------
- Brief survey of FFT variations.
- Presents Bruun's method as a polynomial reduction approach where X_k is obtained via x(z) mod (z - w_N^k).
- Notes the algorithm is fast but less accurate than Cooley--Tukey and rarely used in practice.

Algorithmic Views of Vectorized Polynomial Multipliers – NTRU Prime (2023)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Explores vectorized polynomial multiplication for lattice-based cryptography.
- Uses Good–Thomas, Rader's and Bruun FFTs to avoid coefficient doubling when q-1 has limited powers of two.
- Demonstrates significant performance gains on sntrup761 parameters.

Chen & Sorensen 1992
--------------------
- Proposes efficient FFT for real-symmetric data based on Bruun's algorithm.
- Almost all twiddle factors are real except for the last stage.
- Highlights simple data flow and good match with DSP architectures.
